2. Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of data when you use Foodie:

2.1 Account & Contact Information

  • Name — first and last name for your profile
  • Email address — for authentication and transactional communications
  • Phone number — for order updates and delivery confirmation
  • Delivery address — for processing food delivery orders

2.2 Health & Nutrition Data

  • Nutrition data — meals logged, calories, macronutrients (proteins, carbs, fats), and micronutrients you enter manually or via food database
  • Fitness data — activity data if you grant access to Apple Health (optional)

Health and nutrition data is categorized as special category data under GDPR Article 9 and requires your explicit consent.

2.3 Location

  • Precise location — when searching nearby restaurants or confirming delivery (requires your permission)
  • Approximate location — city-level location for regional restaurant suggestions

2.4 Reservation Data

Foodie processes no banking data. Foodie is a matchmaking platform and does not collect, store, or transmit any payment information. Reservations only record:

  • Selected products — quantity, reference, price displayed by the producer (indicative information only)
  • Pickup slot — date and location agreed with the producer
  • Payment method hint — planned means of payment (cash, card on pickup, transfer, check), provided for informational purposes only to help the producer prepare. This indication triggers no automated processing and is not transmitted to any financial provider.
  • Reservation history — for your personal records

2.5 User Content

  • Photos and videos — profile pictures, food photos, restaurant images you upload
  • Reviews and ratings — feedback you submit for restaurants or orders
  • Customer support messages — communications sent to our support team
  • Search history — restaurant and food searches within the app

2.6 Identifiers

  • User ID — account identifier assigned at registration
  • Device ID — used for Game Center integration and multi-device sync

2.7 Usage Data

  • App interactions — screens visited, features used, session duration
  • Gameplay data — scores, progress, and in-app achievements
  • Purchase tendencies — aggregated ordering patterns used to personalize recommendations

2.8 Technical Diagnostics

  • Crash reports — automatically collected to improve app stability
  • Performance data — app launch time and response metrics (not linked to your identity)

2.9 Spatial Data (Apple Vision Pro only)

  • Environment scanning — room mesh used for spatial UI placement (processed locally on-device, never sent to our servers)
  • Hand and head tracking — gesture input for navigation (processed by Apple's ARKit framework on-device)

4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we process your personal data under the following legal bases:

Health and nutrition data Explicit Consent (Art. 9.2.a) — special category; you must actively opt in

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share data only with the following categories of service providers under Data Processing Agreements:

  • Authentication & database — Supabase, running on Amazon Web Services (AWS) — region eu-west-3 (Paris, France)
  • Cloud sync — Apple CloudKit (iCloud infrastructure)
  • Push notifications — Apple Push Notification Service (APNs)
  • Crash reporting — Xcode Organizer / Apple Crash Reports

No payment processing: Foodie does not collect, store, or transmit any banking data. No payment provider (Stripe, Apple Pay, Google Pay, etc.) is used. Payment is handled directly between buyer and producer, outside the application.

We may disclose data if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access — request a copy of your data
  • Right to rectification — correct inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure — request deletion of your account and data
  • Right to portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Right to restriction — limit how we process your data
  • Right to object — object to processing based on legitimate interest
  • Right to withdraw consent — withdraw any consent at any time (does not affect past processing)
  • Right not to be sold (CCPA) — we do not sell your data

To exercise your rights, contact us at privacy@foodie.fr or use the in-app account deletion feature (Settings → Account → Delete Account).

If you are in the EEA, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

8. Children's Privacy

Foodie is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or 16 in certain jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us immediately at privacy@foodie.fr and we will delete it.

9. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • Encryption in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest
  • Authentication tokens with automatic expiration
  • Row-level security on all database tables
  • iCloud end-to-end encryption for CloudKit sync data
  • Keychain storage for all sensitive credentials on-device

Despite these measures, no system is 100% secure. If you believe your account has been compromised, contact us immediately.

10. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed outside your country of residence. Where we transfer data from the EEA to third countries, we use:

  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission
  • Adequacy decisions where applicable

Supabase offers EU region hosting — contact us if you require your data to remain within the EEA.

11. Apple Platform Privacy

iCloud & CloudKit

Nutrition data and preferences synced via Apple CloudKit are stored in your personal iCloud account and governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. We cannot access your iCloud data without your explicit permission.

HealthKit

If you connect Apple Health, data flows from HealthKit are governed by your iOS Health app permissions. We only read data you explicitly authorize. HealthKit data is never used for advertising.

Game Center

Leaderboards and achievements use Apple Game Center. Your Game Center alias and scores are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Apple Vision Pro (visionOS)

Environment scanning, hand tracking, and eye tracking on Apple Vision Pro are processed exclusively by Apple's ARKit and visionOS frameworks on your device. This data is never transmitted to our servers.

App Tracking Transparency

Foodie does not track you across other apps or websites. We do not use your Advertising Identifier (IDFA) for advertising purposes.

12. Changes to This Policy

Your continued use of Foodie after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
