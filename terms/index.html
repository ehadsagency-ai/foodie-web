2. Description of Service

Foodie is a multi-platform food application available on iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, Apple TV, Apple Vision Pro, and Mac. The App provides:

  • Food Marketplace — Browse, order, and pay for food from local restaurants and vendors
  • Nutrition Tracker — Log meals, track calories, macros, and sync with Apple HealthKit
  • P2P Delivery — Peer-to-peer order confirmation via QR code scan and NFC tap
  • Social Reviews — Share photos of dishes and write reviews for restaurants
  • Live Cooking (visionOS) — Spatial cooking experiences and 3D product visualization
  • Gamification — Achievements, leaderboards, and rewards via Game Center

The App is provided on a freemium basis. Core features are available for free; premium features require a paid subscription.
