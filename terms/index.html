3. Purpose and Nature of the Service

3.1 Matchmaking and hosting status

Foodie is a matchmaking service within the meaning of Article 6.I.2 of French law n°2004-575 of June 21, 2004 ("LCEN") and Article 14 of the EU e-Commerce Directive (2000/31/EC). The Operator acts as a hosting intermediary for content published by Producers and does not exercise any prior control over this content.

The App offers the following features:

  • Geolocated directory of local Producers with an interactive map;
  • Seasonality calendar and indicative carbon scores;
  • Direct messaging between Buyer and Producer;
  • Submission and confirmation of basket or product reservation requests;
  • Physical pickup validation via double P2P confirmation (NFC / QR code);
  • History of past reservations;
  • Optional consumption assistance tools: food recognition, HealthKit nutrition tracking, menu translation.

3.2 Foodie is not a party to transactions

Foodie is a mere technical intermediary. The Operator is at no point a party to the sale contract concluded between the Buyer and the Producer. The sale contract is formed directly between these two parties, outside the App.

3.3 No payment processing

No financial transaction is processed, initiated, facilitated, stored, or routed by the App. Payment takes place directly between the Buyer and the Producer, according to the terms agreed between them (cash, producer's card terminal, bank transfer, check, or any other legal means of payment), outside the App. Foodie does not issue any invoice, does not collect any commission, does not handle or transfer any funds, and holds no banking information about Users.

The indication of a payment method in the App ("cash", "card on pickup", "bank transfer", etc.) is purely informational, intended to help the Producer prepare appropriate means of collection. This indication does not engage any automated payment processing by Foodie.

8. In-App Order Flow

8.1 Reservation request

The Buyer expresses a purchase intent by selecting products and a pickup slot. This request is an expression of intent transmitted to the Producer; it does not legally bind Foodie and does not constitute a firm order until the Producer confirms it.

8.2 Confirmation by the Producer

The Producer accepts or refuses the request. Upon acceptance, the sale contract is formed directly between the Buyer and the Producer, outside the App. Foodie is not a party to this contract.

8.3 Pickup validation (P2P NFC / QR code)

At the time of pickup, the Buyer and Producer physically validate the handover through an NFC or QR code double-confirmation mechanism. This validation only serves to mark the reservation as "picked up" in the App; it does not imply any transfer of funds and does not engage Foodie beyond keeping a history entry.

8.4 Cancellation, rejection, no-show

The Producer may reject a request before acceptance. The Buyer may cancel their request before the Producer confirms it. In the case of a no-show, the Producer may mark the reservation as "no-show"; Foodie is limited to recording this status.

8.5 Disputes between parties

Any dispute regarding quality, conformity, payment, absence of product, or any other commercial matter shall be settled directly between the Buyer and the Producer. Foodie may, purely optionally, facilitate dialogue via its messaging but is not required to resolve the dispute and does not provide any compensation.
