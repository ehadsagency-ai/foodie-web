1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. Foodie is a native iOS application and does not use browser cookies. Instead, we use equivalent technologies such as local storage and device identifiers to provide core app functionality.

2. Technologies We Use

TechnologyPurposeDuration

3. No Third-Party Advertising Cookies

Foodie does not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels. We do not share your data with advertising networks. No IDFA (Advertising Identifier) is collected.

4. Your Choices

You can control data collection preferences in the app under Settings → Privacy. You can disable analytics at any time. Disabling analytics does not affect core app functionality.
